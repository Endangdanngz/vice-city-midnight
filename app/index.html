Trophy names, descriptions and grades are the official PlayStation 5 list as published on PlayStation Network, via Exophase and PSNProfiles. Every guide is written in our own words and cites its sources beside the trophy; further reading: GTA-Zone, Output Lag and PlanetGTA6. Where no source has pinned a trophy down yet, the guide says so instead of guessing.
